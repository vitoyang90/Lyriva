# Lyriva App Icon

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. The mark combines a rounded L with two audio-wave strokes in lime on a dark green-black background. No wordmark is embedded, so the icon remains legible at small sizes.

- Generated master: `design/lyriva-icon-original.png`
- iOS icon: `SpotifyTrans/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/Lyriva-Icon.png` (1024 × 1024, opaque RGB)
- In-app brand asset: `SpotifyTrans/Assets.xcassets/LyrivaMark.imageset/Lyriva-Mark.png`
- The generated master is resized only for asset packaging; no visual redesign is applied during export.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iOS app icon for Lyriva, a modern music companion expanding from lyrics and pronunciation into a broader music experience. Generate exactly one square 1024x1024 image, opaque full-bleed, with square outer corners (iOS will mask it). Background: uniform very dark green-black #0C1113. Subject: a distinctive abstract capital L fused with an audio wave, one cohesive bold geometric symbol. The main upright of the L is a tall softly rounded vertical stroke on the left that turns through a smooth generous inner corner into a horizontal foot extending right. Two shorter upright rounded wave strokes rise progressively on the right above the foot, separated with generous dark negative space, conveying rhythm. It must read first as a strong custom L monogram and second as sound in motion, not a generic equalizer or Spotify logo. Color: fresh muted luminous lime #CAF279 for the complete symbol; subtle ivory highlight is optional but prioritize a clean flat one-color mark. Composition: optically centered, symbol occupies approximately 60% of canvas width and height, generous safe area, immediately legible at 48 pixels. Style: precise minimalist geometric brand mark, premium restrained music app, smooth clean contours, balanced custom geometry. No written brand name, no wordmark, no captions, no tiny detail, no border, no separate rounded-square tile, no shadow, no mockup, no grain, no texture, no 3D, no bevel, no neon glow, no music note, no headphones, no Spotify curved-wave emblem. Deliver only the final icon artwork.
